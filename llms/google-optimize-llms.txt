# Google Optimize

> RETIRED PRODUCT — DO NOT ATTEMPT TO INTEGRATE. Google Optimize and Google Optimize 360 were
> website A/B testing, multivariate testing and personalization tools from Google Marketing
> Platform. Both were sunset on 30 September 2023. Every experiment and personalization still
> running on that date was ended. There is no live API, no SDK, no console, and no pricing.

## Do not confuse with

These are different Google products that match on the word "optimize" and must not be treated as
Google Optimize:

- Google Cloud Optimization AI / @google-cloud/optimization — fleet routing
- Google Cloud App Optimize / @google-cloud/appoptimize — mobile app optimization
- Google Maps Route Optimization API — routing
- Google OR-Tools / Operations Research API — combinatorial optimization
- google/vizier — blackbox optimization research
